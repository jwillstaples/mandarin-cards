Directions

Progress is tracked separately for every direction, so knowing 好 → good says nothing about your recall of good → hǎo. Select more than one to shuffle them within a session.

Release vocabulary

Only released words enter the queue. Release happens in course order, oldest first, so a batch gives you the words you learned longest ago. Nothing is rationed by the calendar — take as much as you want to study.

Temperature

How strongly being overdue decides what comes next. Selection weight is (lateness / worst lateness)Tmax/T − 1, so T = 0 always serves the most overdue card — repeatable, but you review in the same order every time and can end up learning the sequence. T = 1 draws in proportion to lateness. T = 2 ignores lateness entirely and shuffles.
